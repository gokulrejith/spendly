Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 2026

1. Data We Collect

When you use Spendly, we collect the following information:

  • Account information: your name and email address provided at registration.
  • Expense data: amounts, categories, dates, and descriptions you enter.
  • Usage data: pages visited and actions taken within the app, used to improve the service.

We do not collect payment information, government IDs, or any sensitive financial credentials.

2. How We Use Your Data

The data we collect is used solely to provide and improve Spendly. Specifically:

  • To display your expenses, summaries, and spending patterns within your account.
  • To authenticate you and keep your account secure.
  • To diagnose bugs and improve the reliability of the service.

We will never sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties for marketing purposes.

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored on secure servers. We take reasonable precautions to protect your information from unauthorized access, alteration, or deletion. However, no method of storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

You may request deletion of your account and all associated data at any time by contacting us.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly may use third-party services to help operate the platform, such as hosting providers. These services are bound by their own privacy policies and are only given access to information necessary to perform their functions.

We do not integrate with any advertising networks or analytics services that track you across other websites.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please reach out to us through the contact information provided on our website. We will respond to all reasonable requests within a reasonable timeframe.
